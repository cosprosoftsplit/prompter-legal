Prompter: Teleprompter Video

A teleprompter for iPhone that records the take while you read.

On your device, and nowhere else

Prompter has no account, no sign-in and no server component — it contains no networking code at all. Scripts and recorded takes are stored in the app's protected data area on your device. Photo library access is add-only: the app can hand a finished take to your library and cannot read what is already in it.
